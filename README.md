### 📊 About & Focus Areas

I specialize in extracting clarity from complex raw telemetry and translating data into strategic decisions:

* 📐 **Data Modeling & Warehousing**: Designing robust Star / Snowflake schemas, Medallion architectures (Bronze ➔ Silver ➔ Gold), and normalized staging environments.
* 📈 **Business Intelligence & Dashboards**: Developing executive Power BI & Streamlit reports with advanced DAX measures, retention cohorts, and KPI tracking.
* 🔍 **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: Cleaning, transforming, and profiling data using modern Python (Pandas, PyArrow, NumPy) and SQL.
* ⚙️ **ETL & Data Pipelines**: Automating reliable extraction, validation, anomaly quarantine, and scheduled data loading.
